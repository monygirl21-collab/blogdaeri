About (English)

If You Were Born Again is an interactive web experience that lets people draw a different life weighted by real statistics: a past life drawn from the roughly 104.5 billion people born before 1900, a next life drawn from the population of animals alive today, or a birth in Joseon-era Korea drawn from historical household registers. Every result shows the real statistic behind it.

Today each result is assembled from statistics and short pre-written story fragments. We plan to use Claude to turn each draw into a richer, personal short story while keeping the underlying statistics accurate and clearly separated from fiction.

Operated by VIVAFRANG, a Korean retail business founded in 2023. Contact: hello@blogdaeri.xyz

이 사이트는 실제 통계 비율을 바탕으로 한 재미용 추첨입니다. 실제 전생, 운명, 신분과는 아무 관련이 없습니다. 이야기 속 세부 사건은 통계가 아니라 그 시대를 떠올리게 하려고 지어낸 장면입니다.
